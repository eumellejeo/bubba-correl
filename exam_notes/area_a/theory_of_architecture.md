## PART 1: EASY QUESTIONS (Questions 1 to 20)

#### 1. According to Francis D.K. Ching's spatial hierarchy, what fundamental primary element has position but lacks length, width, and depth, acting as the prime generator of all form?

A. Plane  
B. Line  
C. Point  
D. Volume

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Technical Rationale:** A point marks a position in space and is conceptually dimensionless, static, and directionless. When extended linearly, a point becomes a line; when a line is extended laterally, it generates a plane. Examinees commonly confuse the _conceptual generator_ (point) with its two-dimensional manifestation (plane).

- **Correct Answer:** B

#### 20. In sun shading calculations, what is the fundamental mathematical definition of the Horizontal Shadow Angle (HSA)?

A. The sum of the solar altitude and the window head height.  
B. The angular difference between the solar azimuth and the orientation (wall azimuth) of the building face considered.  
C. The vertical inclination of the sun relative to the window sill plane.  
D. The total daily hours of direct beam radiation striking an unshaded facade.

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Technical Rationale:** \(\text{HSA} = \text{AZI} - \text{ORI}\), where \(\text{AZI}\) is the sun's solar azimuth and \(\text{ORI}\) is the wall normal orientation of the specific building facade. Candidates frequently confuse HSA with the vertical shadow angle (VSA) or assume it is derived from solar altitude.

#### 21. An architect is calculating the solar geometry for an exterior fenestration on a building face oriented toward azimuth \(\text{ORI} = 225^\circ\) (Southwest). At 2:00 PM on May 16, the solar azimuth is determined from the sun path diagram to be \(\text{AZI} = 277^\circ\). What is the resulting Horizontal Shadow Angle (HSA), and on which side of the wall normal does the sun strike?

A. \(-52^\circ\), striking counter-clockwise from the wall normal  
B. \(+52^\circ\), striking clockwise from the wall normal  
C. \(+60^\circ\), striking perpendicular to the wall normal  
D. \(-25^\circ\), striking parallel to the wall face

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Technical Rationale:** Using the formula \(\text{HSA} = \text{AZI} - \text{ORI}\), we calculate \(\text{HSA} = 277^\circ - 225^\circ = +52^\circ\). By standard convention, when \(\text{AZI} > \text{ORI}\), the HSA value is positive, indicating that the sun is positioned clockwise relative to the outward normal of the building face.

#### 22. Given a facade where the computed Horizontal Shadow Angle is \(\text{HSA} = 52^\circ\) and the solar altitude angle is \(\text{ALT} = 60^\circ\), what is the exact Vertical Shadow Angle (VSA) required to size a continuous horizontal overhang?

A. \(60.00^\circ\)  
B. \(52.00^\circ\)  
C. \(70.43^\circ\)  
D. \(45.18^\circ\)

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Technical Rationale:** The Vertical Shadow Angle is governed by the trigonometric expression \(\text{VSA} = \arctan\left(\frac{\tan\text{ALT}}{\cos\text{HSA}}\right)\). Substituting the values: \(\tan 60^\circ \approx 1.73205\) and \(\cos 52^\circ \approx 0.61566\), resulting in \(\frac{1.73205}{0.61566} \approx 2.8133\); \(\arctan(2.8133) \approx 70.43^\circ\). A common trap is assuming that VSA equals the solar altitude (\(\text{ALT} = 60^\circ\)), which only occurs when sunlight strikes perpendicular to the facade (\(\text{HSA} = 0^\circ\)).
