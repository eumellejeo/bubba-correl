## PART 1: EASY QUESTIONS (Questions 1 to 20)

### 1.

Under Republic Act No. 9266 (The Architecture Act of 2004) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, what is the mandatory minimum fine imposed on any person convicted of practicing or offering to practice architecture in the Philippines without being registered/licensed? A. PHP 50,000.00  
B. PHP 100,000.00  
C. PHP 200,000.00  
D. PHP 500,000.00

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Technical Rationale:** Section 29 of RA 9266 establishes that illegal practice of architecture or misdemeanor violations carry a fine of not less than PHP 100,000.00 nor more than PHP 5,000,000.00, or imprisonment of 6 months to 6 years, or both. Candidates frequently confuse this general penal clause with Section 30, which penalizes persons who coerce or force an architect to perform services without a written agreement, carrying a higher minimum threshold of PHP 200,000.00.

### 2.

Under Section 30 of RA 9266, any individual or entity that compels, coerces, or forces a registered architect to render architectural services without a signed service agreement or contract shall be guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not less than: A. PHP 100,000.00  
B. PHP 150,000.00  
C. PHP 200,000.00  
D. PHP 500,000.00

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Technical Rationale:** RA 9266 Section 30 specifically enforces contract execution to safeguard the intellectual and economic rights of the professional, penalizing violators with a fine of not less than PHP 200,000.00 or imprisonment not exceeding 6 years. The board exam distractor is PHP 100,000.00, which is the baseline fine for general unlicensed practice under Section 29.

### 10.
